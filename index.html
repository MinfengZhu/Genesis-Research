The evolution of mathematical reasoning.

Genesis Research combines language models with formal proof checking. Our first goal is Olympiad-level geometry.

01 · Mission

Mathematics needs a new kind of reasoning

Machines calculate fast. They still struggle with judgment. Our systems pair a language model, which suggests ideas, with a symbolic engine, which checks every one. Can mathematical reasoning become a procedure that a machine can follow? Mathematicians have asked this for a hundred years.

we are here
  1. 1920s

    Hilbert's program

    Hilbert asks for mathematics in exact formal rules, with a procedure that can decide any statement.

  2. 1931

    Gödel

    No consistent formal system that covers arithmetic can prove every true statement. The full dream fails.

  3. 1951

    Tarski

    Elementary geometry is different. A mechanical procedure can decide every statement, but it is far too slow.

  4. 1970s

    Wu Wen-tsun

    Wu turns geometry into algebra that a computer can check. He calls it Mathematics Mechanization.

  5. Today

    Genesis Research

    Machines check facts well but choose constructions poorly. We add learned judgment: a model proposes, a symbolic engine verifies.

吾生也有涯，而知也无涯。以有涯随无涯，殆已！

Our life has a limit, but knowledge has none. To chase the limitless with the limited is perilous.
