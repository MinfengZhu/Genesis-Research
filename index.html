Our Mission

Mathematics needs a new kind of reasoning

Machines can calculate fast. They still struggle with judgment. Judgment means looking at a diagram, sensing which step might unlock a proof, and knowing when an argument holds up. We build systems with this kind of judgment. A language model suggests ideas. A symbolic engine checks each one. Every idea gets checked before we trust it.

01

Verifiable by Design

A symbolic engine checks every proof we produce. We only publish results we can verify.

02

Grounded in Intuition

Diagrams carry real information. Our models learn to read shapes and symmetry, the way mathematicians do.

03

Compounding Knowledge

Every solved proof teaches us something new. Our systems learn from their own work and improve over time.
